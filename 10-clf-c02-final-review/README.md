## Exam-Day Strategy

When answering questions:

1. Identify the business requirement.
2. Look for keywords.
3. Eliminate incorrect services.
4. Prefer AWS-managed solutions when possible.
5. Choose the simplest AWS-native answer that satisfies the requirement.

---

## Readiness Assessment

You are likely ready for CLF-C02 if:

* You can explain all four exam domains.
* You recognize common AWS services immediately.
* You completed the hands-on exercises in this guide.
* You understand AWS pricing fundamentals.
* You can navigate the AWS Console confidently.
